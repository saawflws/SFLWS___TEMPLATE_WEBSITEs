01 — FIELD NOTES

EST. 2020 · IN

I'm Aasim — a graduate engineer from Hyderabad who ships end to end. My work sits at the intersection of product engineering and applied machine learning: I've taken ideas from whiteboard sketch to production deploys across React/Next.js frontends, Node and FastAPI services, and inference pipelines that hold up under real load.

A less-travelled part of my trail: Zoho CRM and automation architecture. I've designed Deluge-driven systems for logistics and sales teams — custom modules, approval chains, integrations — where a well-placed automation is worth a hire. I think of CRM work like cartography: the value is in mapping how a business actually moves.

Beneath the web layer I keep a systems interest burning — Rust, Go, and C++, compilers, memory models, and the strange satisfaction of shaving milliseconds. It makes me a better product engineer: you build differently once you've seen what the machine is doing for you.

Off the clock you'll find me studying topographic maps for no practical reason, rebuilding my mechanical keyboard layout, and maintaining a graveyard of half-finished experiments I affectionately call "field research."
