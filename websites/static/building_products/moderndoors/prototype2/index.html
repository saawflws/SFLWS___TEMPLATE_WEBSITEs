Shop capabilities

Manufacturing services

Outsource precision cutting, edgebanding, custom panel layup, and specialized gables to free up your shop floor.

Cut & Tape Program

Send your cut-list. We optimize yields with beam saws and apply 1mm or 2mm edgebanding using PUR adhesive.

  • Zero-gap moisture-proof glue line
  • Precise corner rounding & beveling
  • Bulk discounts for cabinet runs

Lamination & Layup

Hydraulic press layup of HPL, wood veneers, PET films, and 3D foils onto plywood, fire-rated substrates, MDF, and particleboard.

  • Thermofoil onto HPL backing
  • Sheets up to 5' × 10' capacity
  • Industrial cold & hot press

Folding Shelves & Gables

Miter-folded hollow-core floating shelves, waterfall island end gables, and heavy structural panels with continuous grain wrapping.

  • 45° seamless waterfall folds
  • 1.5" up to 4" thicknesses
  • Internal mounting support
