Product lines

six systems · one shop · Surrey, BC

How an order runs

Same three steps whether it's one replacement door or a full kitchen. You approve samples before we cut — no surprises at install.

  1. 1

    Send your cut list

    Email an Excel sheet or our order form. List sizes, quantities, finish codes, edge profiles and hinge boring. Unsure about a code? Send a photo of the board label and we'll match it.

  2. 2

    Approve the samples

    We courier physical samples cut from the same batch material as your order. You sign off on color and fit before anything is machined.

  3. 3

    We build and deliver

    The full batch ships from Surrey, crated and corner-taped. Will-call at the shop door, or delivery across Metro Vancouver and the Fraser Valley.
