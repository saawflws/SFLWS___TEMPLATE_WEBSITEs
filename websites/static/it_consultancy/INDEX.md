# it_consultancy — templates

Sites for IT / software / digital-solutions consultancies and technology partners — firms that
map a client's process and then build platform configuration (Zoho), custom apps, marketplaces
and automation. Both templates below are for the same real company (Sanjivini Digital Solutions)
and share its services and flagship product, so pick on **temperature and how the flagship
product is shown**, not on business model.

Read the template's `META.md` before opening its `index.html`.

| Template | Path | Tags | Summary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Sanjivini — Business first, technology next** | `sanjivinifirst/` | `scroll-hue-drift` `flow-field-particle-hero` `live-device-composition` `fraunces-serif-display` | The warm, atmospheric one. Accent hue drifts blue→emerald on scroll, an interactive particle hero, and a LocalFarmers device composition with a live e-Bazar/eStore toggle. Tailwind + GSAP/Lenis + Lucide. |
| **Sanjivini — Business-first technology partners** | `sanjivinipartners/` | `blueprint-grid` `self-drawing-svg-flowchart` `pinned-horizontal-showcase` `pure-css-device-mockups` | The cool, engineering-blueprint one. A self-drawing SVG flowchart hero, cyan→emerald accent, and a pinned one-surface-per-panel LocalFarmers showcase in pure CSS. More editorial sections; degrades gracefully if CDNs fail. |

## Choosing between them

| If the consultancy... | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Wants a warm, premium, design-studio mood with scroll-reactive colour | `sanjivinifirst` |
| Wants a cool, technical, "we draw you the system" blueprint identity | `sanjivinipartners` |
| Wants its flagship product shown as one overlapping, interactive device scene | `sanjivinifirst` |
| Wants its flagship walked through surface-by-surface as dependency-free mockups | `sanjivinipartners` |
| Needs the page to still show content if the animation CDNs fail | `sanjivinipartners` |
