04 / Belonging

We don't sell memberships. We build people. Every rep is a contract with your future self. The iron doesn't lie—neither do we.

Ironhouse was founded in 2014 in a 90-square-meter basement in Shibuya. Three coaches, one barbell, a stack of fractional plates. The mission was simple: build a place where the work comes first, the ego comes last, and the person next to you is a teammate, not a mirror.

Eleven years later, we operate across three cities. Forty-seven coaches. Twelve thousand members who have walked through our doors and chosen to stay. Our 12-month retention is 98%—not because we make it hard to leave, but because people don't quit things that work.

You don't join Ironhouse for the equipment. You join because you're tired of training alone. You stay because the person on the platform next to you noticed you skipped Tuesday—and asked about it on Wednesday. That's belonging. That's the point.
