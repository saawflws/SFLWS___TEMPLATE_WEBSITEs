Build a dark, iron-and-ember landing page for FORGE — a private strength & conditioning studio run by a single founding coach, Marcus Reeve ("One coach. One standard. Zero shortcuts."). The page sells four coaching services rather than gym memberships, and reads like a serious personal-coaching practice.

Visual Strategy:
- Near-black canvas with a single hot ember-red accent and a bone off-white for text.
- Ultra-condensed Anton display type, monospaced technical labels, a fine grain/noise overlay and a top scroll-progress bar.
- Desaturated, high-contrast photography with a red duotone/graphic treatment on the hero image.

Color Palette:
- Layered blacks: near-black page, coal and iron for cards and panels.
- Bone primary text, ash grey for supporting copy.
- One hot red accent (with a deeper red for depth) for buttons, headings-on-hover, the marquee band and links.

Typography:
- Anton for display and headings — condensed all-caps.
- Archivo for body copy.
- JetBrains Mono for labels, eyebrows and metadata.

Page Structure:
- Fixed nav with a "Start Now" button and a full-screen overlay menu.
- Hero: "Forge your best self." with the coach's status ("accepting new clients"), a founder badge and count-up stats.
- A red marquee band of values.
- Method: the coach's philosophy, a portrait, and a strip of counters.
- Programs: four service offers (One-on-One Coaching, Online Programs, Nutrition Planning, Group Training) as hoverable list rows with per-session / per-month pricing.
- A full-bleed statement / creed section.
- Mindset: a horizontally-scrolling "library" of articles that open in a reader modal.
- Proof: two rows of scrolling client testimonials with results.
- CTA / Contact: "Ready to be forged?" with a free-session request form.
- Footer: sitemap, hours, contact and a giant FORGE watermark.

Interaction Details:
- A SplitText loader, a choreographed hero intro, Lenis smooth scroll and heavy GSAP/ScrollTrigger animation throughout.
- A custom cursor (dot, trailing ring and contextual "view" label) and magnetic buttons.
- Pinned horizontal mindset library, scroll-velocity-reactive marquee, parallax media, count-up statistics.
- An article reader modal and a contact form modal that pre-selects the chosen service, both with inline validation and demo success states.
- Image error fallbacks to grayscale placeholders; reduced-motion path that renders everything static.

Overall Vibe: disciplined, premium, editorial and coach-led — a one-coach strength practice presented with fashion-brand production values.
