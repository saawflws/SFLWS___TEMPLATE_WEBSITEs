# boutique_yoga_studio — templates

Calm, earthy studio one-pagers — the deliberate opposite of the `gym` category. Both share a
terracotta-and-sage palette on warm paper; they differ in production level and voice.

Read the template's `META.md` before opening its `index.html`.

| Template | Path | Tags | Summary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **MORROW** | `morrow/` | `serif-on-serif` `quiet-literary` `low-density` `paper-grain-texture` | The hushed one. Serif body copy, no sticky header, no full-bleed photography, everything in normal document flow. Reads like a handwritten sign on the door. |
| **STILLWATER** | `stillwater/` | `editorial-cinematic` `sticky-blurred-header` `full-bleed-hero-image` `persistent-ambient-widgets` | The produced one. Sticky glass header, cinematic full-bleed hero, sans-serif body, and two always-on floating widgets (breathing indicator, sound consent). |

## Choosing between them

| If the studio... | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Is small, new, or values restraint over polish | `morrow` |
| Is established and wants a magazine-grade, art-directed feel | `stillwater` |
| Has strong photography to lead with | `stillwater` |
| Has little or no photography | `morrow` |
