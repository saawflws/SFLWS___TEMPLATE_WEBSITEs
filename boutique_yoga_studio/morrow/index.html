Morrow · Hudson, New York · est. 2019 · eight mats per room

Morrow is a two-room studio on Warren Street. We teach vinyasa, yin, restorative, and breathwork — eight students to a class, by reservation. Mats are laid out before you arrive. The room is warm. You won't be asked to push past anything.

i.practices
01
Vinyasa
Movement linked to breath. We move slowly enough that you can feel what you're doing — six or seven shapes, repeated, held, revisited.60 minutes · all levels
02
Yin
Long, passive holds. Five minutes per shape. We use bolsters, blocks, and the wall. Mostly floor work, mostly silence.75 minutes · all levels
03
Restorative
Six or seven shapes, fully supported by props. You'll spend most of class on the floor. The hardest part is staying awake.75 minutes · all levels
04
Breathwork
Three-part breath, box breathing, and occasional bhramari. Forty-five minutes, seated. No postures. We provide chairs and cushions.45 minutes · no experience needed

Your first
class is on us.

Leave your name and email — we'll write back within a day with three available times that fit your week, and answer any questions you have. No automated booking flow. Maren or James reads every note.

We don't sell class packs. After your first class, drop-in is $22, or $180 for a ten-class pass. We don't do memberships, contracts, or auto-renew. You just come when you want to come.
