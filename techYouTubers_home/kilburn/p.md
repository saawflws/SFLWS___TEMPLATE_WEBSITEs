Design a tech YouTuber's home website, complementing video content with deep-dive written reviews.

Visual Strategy:
Imagery: video thumbnails, behind-the-scenes shots, creator portrait.
Photography: signature high-contrast lighting.
Composition: cinematic 16:9 hero blocks.

Color Palette:
Primary Colors: deep black, signal red, pure white.
Accent Colors: neon yellow, electric blue.
Background: pure black with film-grain overlay.

Typography:
Headings: heavy display sans.
Body Text: clean grotesque.
Layout: video-first with collapsible written companion.

Page Structure:
Hero Section: latest video as autoplay-on-hover thumbnail.
Video Library: chronological with category filters.
Written Reviews: longer-form companions to videos.
Recommendations: "What I'd Buy at $X" guides.
Newsletter & Patreon.

Interaction Details:
- Video thumbnails play silent muted previews on hover.
- Filters slide cards into a new arrangement with a 300ms shuffle.
- Written reviews have a "read along while video plays" sticky video.
- Recommendation cards have a price-range slider that re-sorts in real time.
- Newsletter signup uses a typewriter-style placeholder cycling through pitches.
- Page uses scroll-driven brand color shifts at section breaks.

Overall Vibe: high-production, opinionated, charismatic, modern.
